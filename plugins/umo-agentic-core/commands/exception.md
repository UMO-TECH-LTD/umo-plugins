---
description: Record an exception to a core invariant or adoption-mode requirement.
---

Use exceptions sparingly.

Record:

- invariant or requirement being excepted;
- scope and expiry;
- owner approving the exception;
- reason and risk;
- evidence that normal compliance is not practical yet;
- follow-up bead, issue, or review date.

Exceptions cannot silently weaken core behavior for a whole repo. They must be
explicit, scoped, and revisited.
